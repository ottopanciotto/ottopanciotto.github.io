# Otto’s Adventure Journal

A playful, illustrated editorial journal for a curious monkey. Use asymmetric spreads, large photographs, travel stamps, and clear, conversational first-person copy. Existing character scenes are illustrations, not documentary evidence.

- Voice: Otto speaks as a curious kid. Use concrete details and keep his opinions and occasional jokes. Avoid repeated slogans, mock departments, paperwork jokes, and a punchline after every fact. Label imagined scenes clearly.
- Palette: cream `#f7f1e5`, ink `#24291e`, burnt orange `#ae4120`, bright orange `#ed7344` for surfaces, blue `#244ac0`, navy `#172f55`, yellow `#f5ca53`.
- Typography: self-hosted Outfit headings, Work Sans body. Body text 16px by default; compact card descriptions 13–14px. No external font requests.
- Layout: editorial grid, generous section spacing, restrained frame rotation. Single-column cards on phones; two columns on tablets; three on wide screens, with one featured crew spread.
- Interactions: native disclosures, real buttons and anchors, visible focus, minimum 44px controls. No hover-only content.
- Motion: brief entrance and hover feedback; respect reduced motion. Content remains visible without animation.
- Travel: curated public notes ordered by starting month, newest first. Cross-year journeys appear once; overlapping route records are combined. Do not infer exact days. Totals and filters come from the same dataset.
- Imagery: reuse character artwork with responsive WebP, explicit dimensions, and lazy loading below the hero. Decorative SVGs stay hidden from assistive technology.

Based on UI/UX Pro Max editorial-grid guidance, adapted to the approved adventure-journal direction.
