01 / Meet the monkeyA short introduction

Eight teeth.
One little belly.
That’s me.

Otto is for my eight-tooth smile. Panciotto is for my little belly. The name fits.

I was born around 2014, but my real birthday is a mystery. The date I celebrate is 21 January 2017: the day I found my family in Liguria.

Mom gave me my personality. Dad helped me find my voice. At home in Stockholm, I learn through experiments and stories, with breaks for snacks.

There’s a lot to learn when you’re a little monkey.
